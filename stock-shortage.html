PROJECT CASE STUDY

Stock Shortage Analysis

A shortage-analysis project focused on identifying recurring material gaps and supporting purchase planning. ### Business question Which materials are repeatedly short, by how much, and during which periods? ### Analysis - Compare required quantity with available stock. - Calculate shortage quantity and shortage percentage. - Rank materials by shortage frequency. - Analyze shortage by month and category. - Create an action list for procurement. ### Tools Excel and SQL. ### Interview story “The goal is to move from simply reporting a shortage to identifying patterns that can improve purchase planning.”
