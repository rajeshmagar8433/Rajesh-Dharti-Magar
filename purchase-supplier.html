PROJECT CASE STUDY

Purchase & Supplier Analysis

A procurement analysis project designed to compare supplier pricing, delivery performance and purchase history to support better purchasing decisions.

Business Question

Which suppliers provide the best combination of price, delivery performance and reliability, and where can purchasing decisions be improved?

Objective

Create an analytical reporting system that compares supplier performance, purchase costs and delivery patterns so that procurement teams can make more informed supplier and purchasing decisions.

Data Fields

Supplier, material, category, purchase date, quantity purchased, unit price, freight cost, total purchase cost, promised delivery date, actual delivery date, lead time and purchase order status.

Analysis

  • Compare supplier prices for the same material.
  • Calculate total purchase cost including freight.
  • Compare supplier lead times and delivery performance.
  • Analyze purchase volume and spending by supplier.
  • Identify high-cost and frequently purchased materials.
  • Rank suppliers based on cost and delivery performance.
  • Identify opportunities for better procurement decisions.

Key KPIs

  • Total purchase value
  • Average purchase price
  • Average supplier lead time
  • On-time delivery rate
  • Purchase quantity by supplier
  • Top suppliers by purchase value
  • Top purchased materials
  • Freight cost contribution

Tools Used

Excel for data preparation, formulas, PivotTables and supplier comparison, SQL for querying purchase and supplier data, and Power BI for dashboard development and reporting.

Business Value

The analysis helps convert purchasing data into actionable procurement information. It gives management better visibility into supplier costs, delivery performance and purchasing patterns, helping the team evaluate suppliers and identify opportunities to improve purchasing decisions.

Interview Story

“I built this project to analyze procurement decisions from both cost and supplier-performance perspectives. Instead of looking only at the purchase price, I considered freight, delivery time and purchase history to create a more complete view of supplier performance and support data-driven procurement decisions.”
