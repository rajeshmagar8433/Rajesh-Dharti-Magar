PROJECT CASE STUDY

Purchase & Supplier Analysis

A procurement analytics project for comparing suppliers and supporting purchasing decisions. ### Business question Which supplier provides the best overall commercial option after considering price, transport and delivery terms? ### Analysis - Compare quoted unit prices. - Include transport and other applicable charges. - Compare lead time and availability. - Track historical purchase rates. - Build a supplier comparison scorecard. ### Tools Excel and Power BI. ### Interview story “I separate the quoted price from the effective landed cost so purchasing decisions are based on the real commercial picture.”
