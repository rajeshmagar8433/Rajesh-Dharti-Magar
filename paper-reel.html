PROJECT CASE STUDY

Paper Reel & Consumption Tracker

An operations dashboard for tracking paper reel dimensions, weights, reservations and consumption. ### Business question How can the store team maintain a clear view of usable paper stock and reserved material? ### Analysis - Track reel size and weight. - Record receipts and issues. - Separate available and reserved stock. - Summarize monthly consumption. - Flag unusual differences for investigation. ### Tools Excel and Power BI. ### Interview story “This project connects physical inventory with reporting logic, which is important because operational data is only useful when it matches what is actually available on the floor.”
