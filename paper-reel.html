PROJECT CASE STUDY

Paper Reel & Consumption Tracker

An operations reporting project designed to track paper reel dimensions, stock weight, reservations, consumption and material availability for production planning.

Business Question

How can the store team improve visibility into paper reel availability, reservations and consumption to ensure the right material is available for production when required?

Objective

Create a reporting system that provides clear visibility into paper reel stock, reservations and consumption, enabling the store and production teams to identify available material, monitor usage patterns and plan future material requirements.

Data Fields

Reel ID, reel width, reel weight, receipt date, issue date, material status, reserved job, available stock, consumed quantity and monthly consumption.

Analysis

  • Track paper reel size and weight.
  • Calculate available and reserved stock.
  • Compare received, issued and remaining quantities.
  • Track monthly paper consumption.
  • Analyze stock by reel width.
  • Identify reels reserved for specific jobs.
  • Highlight unusual stock differences for investigation.
  • Support production and material planning.

Key KPIs

  • Total paper stock weight
  • Available stock weight
  • Reserved stock weight
  • Monthly consumption
  • Number of reels in stock
  • Stock by reel width
  • Issued quantity
  • Material utilization

Tools Used

Excel for data preparation, formulas, tracking and analysis, and Power BI for dashboard development and reporting.

Business Value

The analysis provides a structured view of paper reel inventory and consumption, helping the store team distinguish available and reserved material, monitor usage patterns and support better production planning.

Interview Story

“I built this project around a practical inventory problem: paper stock needs to be tracked not only by quantity, but also by reel size, weight and job reservation. I created a reporting structure to connect physical reel inventory with consumption and availability, making it easier to understand what material is available, what is reserved and what may require planning.”
