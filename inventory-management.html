PROJECT CASE STUDY

Inventory Management Dashboard

A practical dashboard for monitoring stock position and material movement. ### Business question How can management quickly see what is available, what has been consumed, and which materials may require action? ### Data fields Material, category, opening stock, receipts, issues, closing stock, unit cost, supplier, reorder level and status. ### Analysis - Calculate closing stock and stock value. - Flag materials below reorder level. - Compare opening, receipts and issues. - Show category-wise stock distribution. - Track monthly consumption. ### Tools Excel, Power Query and Power BI. ### Interview story “I designed the dashboard around the decisions a store team needs to make, rather than only displaying raw inventory numbers.”
