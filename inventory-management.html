PROJECT CASE STUDY

Inventory Management Dashboard

An inventory reporting dashboard designed to monitor stock position, material movement, consumption and reorder requirements.

Business Question

How can management quickly understand current stock, material movement and consumption, and identify materials that may require purchasing action?

Objective

Create a clear reporting system that brings inventory data into one view and helps management monitor stock levels, identify potential shortages and support purchase planning.

Data Fields

Material, category, opening stock, receipts, issues, closing stock, unit cost, supplier, reorder level and status.

Analysis

  • Calculate closing stock and inventory value.
  • Identify materials below the reorder level.
  • Compare opening stock, receipts and issues.
  • Analyze category-wise stock distribution.
  • Track monthly material consumption.
  • Highlight materials requiring purchase attention.

Key KPIs

  • Total inventory value
  • Total stock quantity
  • Monthly consumption
  • Materials below reorder level
  • Category-wise stock distribution

Tools Used

Excel for data preparation and analysis, Power Query for data cleaning and transformation, and Power BI for dashboard development and reporting.

Business Value

The dashboard provides a structured view of inventory data so that stock levels, consumption patterns and potential shortages can be reviewed more efficiently and used to support purchasing decisions.

Interview Story

“I designed the dashboard around the decisions a store team needs to make, rather than only displaying raw inventory numbers. The focus was on stock visibility, consumption, reorder requirements and actionable reporting.”
